Question: is the person shown in the thumbnail the same real person as the one

wearing. Ignore everything that is only presentation: background, lighting,
colour grading, how they are cropped, and any graphics or text laid over them.

still be the same person.

- DIFFERENT — it is a different person, or a person who is not in the footage

  different age, with different hair or facial hair, wearing different clothes,

- UNCLEAR — you genuinely cannot tell, for example because the face is turned
  away or too small to judge.
